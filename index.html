Mobile app · calculates without signal

Shop-floor math for pipefitters, boilermakers and welders.

A mobile app, Android first with iOS planned. The math runs on your phone, with or without signal; you need a connection to sign in to your account. In four languages (Spanish, Dutch, English and French) and in metric and imperial units.

What it calculates

Every calculation has its own screen, with its drawing.

The figure to cut or to mark stays at the top of the screen while the values are typed in. Below it come the dimensioned drawing, generated by code from the result, and the intermediate values.

The drawings below are parts of real screens from version 2.0, in development.

Pipework

The pipe to cut between two fittings, with the take-off of each one already deducted. Elbows cut to any angle, rolled elbows, S jogs, manifolds and branches on an elbow, and an estimate of the welding consumables.

  • Cut length
  • Cut an elbow
  • Rolled elbow
  • S jog
  • Manifold
  • Branch on elbow
  • Welding

Offsets

Offsets made with standard elbows cut to the angle the job needs. GeoMont gives the elbow angle, the nipple between the two elbows and the arcs to mark along the back and the throat, with a 2D drawing and, in most tabs, a 3D view.

  • Offset with a cut elbow
  • S-offset with two cut elbows
  • Corner with two elbows
  • Mitered offset
  • Check installed pipework
  • Parallel turn
  • 3D box offset

Branches and cut-outs

Pipe to pipe, pipe to elbow, elbow to pipe and tee with elbow. Elevation and section drawings, the developed template of the branch and of the hole, a table of ordinates to mark the cut without a printer, and a printable template at 1:1 scale.

  • Pipe to pipe
  • Pipe to elbow
  • Elbow to pipe
  • Tee with elbow

Bending

Offsets, rolling offsets, three- and four-bend saddles, 90° bends and large radii. The distance between bends, the shrink and where each mark goes on the tube.

  • Offset
  • Rolling
  • Saddle 3
  • Saddle 4
  • 90° / Arc
  • Radius

Sheet-metal developments

Cones and truncated cones, shells, segmented elbows, square-to-round transitions, reducers, hoppers, screw flights and breeches. The flat pattern is drawn on screen and comes out as a PDF template at 1:1 scale.

  • Bend
  • Cone
  • Truncated cone
  • Shell
  • Elbow
  • Branch
  • Square to round
  • Reducer
  • Hopper
  • Screw flight
  • Breeches

Tables and weights

Pipe, fitting and flange tables to EN/DIN and ASME, bolt data, and the weight of pipes, profiles, tubes and solid bars. Also bolt torque with the tightening order, bolt-circle marking, support spacing and thermal expansion.

  • Pipe
  • Fittings
  • Flanges
  • Bolts
  • Profiles
  • Tubes
  • Solids & plate
  • Units
  • Volume
  • Bolt torque
  • Bolt circle
  • Weight & supports
  • Thermal expansion
  • Workshop inches
